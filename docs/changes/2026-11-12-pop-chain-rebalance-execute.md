## 2026-11-12 — feat(ops): POP execute, verify and restore for re-assigning lessons across one student's contract chain (#5124)

- POP operation `course-contract-chain-rebalance` (#5124, #5162) is now `active`. Execute runs only a dry-run pinned to its digest and approved by the Founder for that request; it locks the chain, plans again under the locks and refuses on any difference, then moves every lesson through the existing contract-reassign service in one transaction (all or nothing). No lesson is cancelled or created and no money row is written. Verify is read-only. Undo is a new approved request with `restore_request_id` (the Pi-local runner offers no POP rollback for this operation); it moves exactly the same lessons back and refuses as a whole, with a per-lesson report, when a moved lesson changed. A failed execute or restore commits no data change (the transaction is rolled back before the failed receipt is written); a request already restored is refused by its receipts (`restore_already_applied`); stored counters must be the derived ones before an execute (`counters_not_derived`); `future_reservation_policy: report` is refused when the chain has an active contract. Not part of any standing or automatic approval. Runbook: `docs/OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md`.
