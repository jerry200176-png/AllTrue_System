- POP operation `course-contract-chain-rebalance` (#5124, #5162) is now `active`. Execute runs only a dry-run pinned to its digest and approved by the Founder for that request; it locks the chain, plans again under the locks and refuses on any difference, then moves every lesson through the existing contract-reassign service in one transaction (all or nothing). No lesson is cancelled or created and no money row is written. Verify is read-only. Undo (POP rollback, or a new approved request with `restore_request_id`) moves exactly the same lessons back and refuses as a whole, with a per-lesson report, when a moved lesson changed. Not part of any standing or automatic approval. Runbook: `docs/OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md`.
