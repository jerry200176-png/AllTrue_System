## 2026-11-04 — fix(scheduling): 老師填寫的評量鎖定該堂老師（in-app #499）
<!-- release-notes: staff_update=staff-2026-11-04-lesson-teacher-locked -->
- Founder 「哪個老師填的要鎖死」「也鎖死」: a non-voided assessment with a teacher-written body (approved or not; blank, placeholder, system back-fill and voided rows do not count) locks the lesson's teacher to its author on every path. `LearningRecordMutableOwnership::protectedRecordForSession` / `assertLessonTeacherUnlocked` is the one rule: director edits, substitute, undo, restore, batch, reassign, schedule writers and the single writer `OccurrenceAssignmentService` refuse with 409 `approved_assessment_teacher_locked` (「這堂已有 X 老師寫的評量，老師不能改；要改請先作廢評量」). Contract-teacher-change pin passes pin the author. The #3824 snapshot-and-clear-on-teacher-change flow (`AssessmentAuthorSnapshot`) is retired; the author-snapshot history endpoint stays read-only for rows already written. The calendar index and `calendarOccurrenceMerge.js` show the author (`teacher_locked_to_author`).
- To correct a mistake: 退回待審 (rollback) an approved record, then 刪除 the record (director); cancelling the session also voids it.
