# Billing correction after receipt reversal

silent_ship: protected billing repair prepared for review; not deployed or production verified.

Unpaid-only count correction guards now evaluate signed payment history per invoice. Fully reversed receipts with matched report/receipt provenance and exactly one full reversal per report no longer block these corrections; split or extra reversal rows fail closed even when their sum matches the receipt; fractional, malformed or out-of-range report amounts cannot establish an exact match; surviving receipts, partial reversals, nonzero stored PaidAmount and inconsistent negative ledger totals and missing/ambiguous reversal links still block correction. Other callers keep the historical guard. The locked count-correction path locks all ledger rows, including reversals, before repeating the guard. Both date-mode correction endpoints retain their historical conservative payment guard; monthly reversal eligibility and authoritative repricing remain deferred under #3763. Original receipt and reversal rows remain intact. Existing pricing, entitlement and invoice-amount restrictions remain in force.

Payment confirmation and rejection guidance now distinguishes a report's amount from the bill obligation. A conflicting pending report stays visible in the entry form, with the entered amount intact in both the collection page and accounting ledger; it must be checked and rejected/replaced when wrong; an over-limit receipt is retained in the form for reconciliation. The server's invoice cap remains unchanged.

No new schema, linked-reissue behavior, obligation-only correction endpoint or PR4132 dependency is included. Synthetic SQL/controller/component tests verify eligible corrections after full reversal, rejection/replacement before confirmation, selected-invoice caps and partial reversal after preview. A real monthly confirm/snapshot/void scenario verifies correction refusal without changing course, invoice, snapshot or payment history, followed by recollection at the original monthly amount. Actual process concurrency and production paths remain unverified.
