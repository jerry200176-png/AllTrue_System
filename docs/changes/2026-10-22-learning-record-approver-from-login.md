## 2026-10-22 — Learning-record reviews record the logged-in reviewer
<!-- release-notes: silent_ship=silent-2026-10-22-learning-record-approver-from-login -->
<!-- silent-reason: audit-integrity hardening; the app already sends the logged-in user's id, so honest use looks the same -->

`approve`, `rollback-approval`, `reject`, `request-changes`, `batch-approve`, `batch-reject`, `batch-request-changes` and `backdoor-approve` wrote `ApprovedBy` (and, for the backfill, `CreatedByUserID`) from the `DirectorID` the client sent, and passed it to `ApprovalSessionSyncService` as the actor of the approval sign-in (`RecordedByUserID`) and of the rollback void and ledger reversal (`VoidedByUserID`, `created_by`). A director could therefore record a colleague as the approver. Every one of these writes now uses the server-verified login (Founder 2026-10-22); `DirectorID` is still accepted but ignored, and is no longer required by `batch-approve` and `backdoor-approve`. Who may review is unchanged (role and campus checks as before). The retired `bulk-backdoor-approve` (410) body got the same change. Existing rows are not rewritten. Regression: `LearningRecordReviewerFromLoginTest`. Rollback is a normal revert.
