## 2026-11-13 — feat(billing): 收據移到正確帳單，後端預覽 (#5113 step 3a, in-app #522)

<!-- silent-reason: 只新增唯讀後端預覽，功能旗標預設關閉，畫面與帳款都沒有變化，教職員目前看不到差異 -->
- New `GET payment-reports/{id}/reassign-targets` and `POST payment-reports/{id}/reassign` (preview only). Both are read-only: they refuse or return the plan (`from`/`to` balances before and after, `needs_role`, `warnings`, `state_hash`) and write nothing. A POST without `preview: true` answers `reassign_apply_unavailable`; applying (reverse the receipt on the wrong bill, record it on the right one, link row, audit) is the next PR.
- Off by default: feature flag `receipt_reassign` (`FEATURE_RECEIPT_REASSIGN`, per-campus override `FEATURE_RECEIPT_REASSIGN_CAMPUS_<id>`). Director limits come from `BILLING_RECEIPT_REASSIGN_DIRECTOR_MAX_AMOUNT` and `BILLING_RECEIPT_REASSIGN_DIRECTOR_MAX_AGE_DAYS`; both default to 0, which means HQ (super_admin) only. At the limit is allowed, above it answers `reassign_needs_super_admin`.
- Campus is checked before any state: the receipt's campus and the target bill's campus must both belong to the caller (403), and the move must stay inside one campus (422 `reassign_cross_campus`, also for super_admin). Balances come from `Invoice::netPaidSql`, never summed by hand.
- Refusal codes: `reassign_disabled`, `reassign_not_confirmed`, `reassign_amount_zero`, `reassign_source_unresolvable`, `reassign_source_not_applied` (409), `reassign_legacy_reversal_unreconciled` (409), `reassign_cross_campus`, `reassign_target_not_found` (404), `reassign_same_bill`, `reassign_target_voided`, `reassign_target_other_owner`, `reassign_course_refunded`, `reassign_refund_or_transfer_exists`, `reassign_pending_report`, `reassign_target_overpay`, `reassign_needs_super_admin`, `reassign_reason_too_long`.
- `PaymentReportController::void` and every ledger writer are unchanged.
