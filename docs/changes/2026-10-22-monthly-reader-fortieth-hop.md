## 2026-10-22 — Monthly lesson reader keeps the root reached on the 40th reschedule hop
<!-- release-notes: silent_ship=silent-2026-10-22-monthly-reader-fortieth-hop -->
<!-- silent-reason: edge-case parity fix in the same-day monthly reader; no visible change for normal chains -->

`ClassSessionController::monthlyLessonRowsByClass()` walks `original_schedule_id` for at most `BackfillScheduleOccurrenceIdentityService::MAX_HOPS` (40) hops. A chain of exactly 40 links reached its root on the last hop and then fell through to "no identity". A row reached on the last hop now gets the same terminal check as inside the loop (frozen identity, else the root's own slot); a row that still has a parent after the limit is not the root, so a longer chain keeps no identity rather than borrowing an intermediate row's time. Shorter chains behave as before. Regressions: `MonthlyCalendarLeaveNoBackfillTest::test_a_reschedule_chain_of_exactly_forty_links_keeps_its_root_slot` and `test_a_reschedule_chain_longer_than_forty_links_keeps_no_identity`. Rollback is a normal revert. Refs #3970.
