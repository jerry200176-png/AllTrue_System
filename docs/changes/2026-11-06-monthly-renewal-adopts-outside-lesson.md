## 2026-11-06 — fix(billing): monthly renewal adopts an attended lesson the previous month wrongly held (in-app #506)
<!-- release-notes: staff_update=staff-2026-11-06-monthly-renewal-adopts-outside-lesson -->
- Monthly renewal no longer stops on an attended lesson dated after the old period's end but inside the new period. When the old month is unpaid, priced at rate x lessons, and has exactly one unpaid invoice with one item, renewal moves the lesson (with its sign-in, learning record and deduction ledger) to the new contract, lowers the old month's charge, lesson count, invoice total and item by that lesson's rate, and records an audit event. Any payment, discount, hourly rate, or several invoices/items still blocks (`adopt_requires_session_rate`, `adopt_source_discounted`, `adopt_source_invoice_ambiguous`). The preview shows a warning with the old month's new charge; the response lists `adopted_session_ids`. No migration.
