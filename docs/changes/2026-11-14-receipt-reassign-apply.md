## 2026-11-14 — feat(billing): 收據移到正確帳單，後端執行 (#5113 step 3b, in-app #522)
<!-- release-notes: silent_ship=silent-2026-11-14-receipt-reassign-apply -->
<!-- silent-reason: 只新增後端執行，功能旗標預設關閉、主任上限預設為 0，還沒有畫面可以按，教職員目前看不到差異 -->
- `POST payment-reports/{id}/reassign` without `preview: true` now applies the move. It needs the preview's `state_hash` (`reassign_state_hash_required`) and a reason (`reassign_reason_required`). In one transaction with row locks (courses, receipt, packages, bills, payments, in confirm()/void() order) it recomputes the plan, refuses a changed state (`reassign_state_changed`, 409), reverses the receipt on the bill its money sits on (a `void` Payment linked to the old receipt and tagged `[receipt_reassign:<id>]`), voids the old receipt, records a new confirmed receipt with its Payment on the target bill, writes one `payment_report_reassignments` row and one `billing.receipt_reassigned` audit event (reason stored as a hash). Original Payment rows are never edited. A target course whose package belongs to another student or campus is refused (`reassign_target_package_mismatch`), and so is a source bill pointing at another student's course (`reassign_source_other_owner`); the hash also covers both projected after-balances; the new receipt keeps the note the old one showed.
- Idempotent: the same report, target and `state_hash` again returns the stored move (200, `replayed: true`); a concurrent twin waits on the locks and replays. Any other plan for a moved receipt is `reassign_state_changed`.
- Both bills' after-balances, in the preview and after the move, come from `InvoiceAmountReconciliationService` (an over-collected source keeps its surplus; a monthly source back at net 0 is repriced). A repriced monthly target is saved at its session total before the money lands, through the step confirm() uses (`MonthlyBillFreeze`, extracted from confirm, behavior unchanged). Course `Paid` and shared-package marks follow void()/confirm() (`PackagePaidMarks`, extracted from PaymentReportController, behavior unchanged).
- `state_hash` now carries a version prefix (`v2:`) and also covers both sides' owner, course and `closed_reason`. Pending-report checks cover every member of a count package on both sides. The director age limit follows the original receipt through earlier moves. (Batching of `reassign-targets` and the pinned not-applied refusal ship in a separate PR.)
- Still off by default (`FEATURE_RECEIPT_REASSIGN`); director limits stay 0 (HQ only) until set in env. `PaymentReportController::void` is unchanged.
