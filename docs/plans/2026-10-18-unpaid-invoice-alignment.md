- **Atomicity:** exact owned bounded graph, preview/rollback-epoch hash and originating POP request; immutable before/after proof, required audit, mutation and outer execute/rollback receipt share one transaction. Rollback refuses downstream drift; interrupted standalone commits can recover through retained proof. Reuse Laravel 8 transaction/savepoints, Ramsey UUIDv5 and existing POP attempt storage; no custom retry framework or dependency.
- **Retries:** serialize request, successful replay lookup and append-only attempt numbering. Failed execute/rollback attempts remain evidence and may retry with valid approval; successful phases replay without another mutation. Other strategies retain their contracts.
- **Delayed recovery:** only these three strategies may append renewed approvals: an eligible execution_failed atomic attempt without proof (no successful execute or rollback history), or manifest-bound original proof without execute receipt, permits execute-recovery; a successful execute permits verify/rollback recovery; any rollback attempt limits renewal to rollback, and successful rollback closes renewal. Recovery tokens cannot authorize a new repair; old successful receipts remain immutable. Complete currently unexpired quorum, original exact commit/parameters and all existing actor/scope/reference/TTL checks remain mandatory. Tokens bind their recovery phase; old approval rows stay unchanged and renewal never queues automatic execute.
- **Evidence:** synthetic real-POP draft/dry-run/dual-approval/execute/verify/rollback, two independent PDO competitors, proof/audit/outer-receipt faults, retained failure history, expired approval/quorum renewal and phase-bound token refusal. RED receipts remain in PR history. Exact-head full CI, independent review, combined-head rehearsal, private case manifest and executable release/backup/recovery evidence remain required before protected delivery.
