# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate professionally, protect customer and student
privacy, and focus review on observable product and engineering outcomes.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data,
and abusive or disruptive conduct are not accepted.

## Engineering conduct

- Never post credentials, production records, personal data, or private
  screenshots in issues, pull requests, logs, or chat.
- Describe risks and disagreements with evidence and actionable alternatives.
- Respect the production control plane and repository safety rules.
- Report security and privacy concerns through `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation
when behavior violates this standard.
